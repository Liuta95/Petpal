About PetPals

We're on a mission to make pet care accessible, trustworthy, and stress-free for pet owners around the world.

Our Story

Founded in 2020, PetPals was born from a simple idea: every pet deserves love and care, even when their owners can't be there.

We started in San Francisco with just 10 sitters and have grown to serve over 50 countries worldwide. Our platform connects passionate pet lovers with families who need reliable, trustworthy care for their furry companions.
